Message delivery

Your message could not be delivered.

NullCord stopped this message before it reached the other person. Here are the most common reasons.

Why did this happen?

  • You are blocked. The person you are trying to message has blocked your account. Blocking also removes any friendship between the two accounts.
  • Privacy settings. The other person only accepts messages from friends or mutual connections.
  • No shared conversation. Direct messages can only be opened when the other person allows messages from you.
  • Server permissions. In a server, a role, timeout or channel override may prevent you from sending messages.
  • Account state. The recipient may be suspended, deleted or otherwise unavailable.

Blocked? A block is one-way. The blocked account can still see your username and profile picture in existing conversations, but cannot view your profile or send you new messages. The block also removes the friendship automatically.

What can I do?

If you believe this is a mistake, contact the person outside NullCord. Only the person who blocked you can remove the block. For server permission issues, ask a server administrator to check your roles and channel overrides.
